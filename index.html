The Unit 40-12

Refrigeration and hot water. One integrated concept.

The Bronzimática Concept Machine is a modular, skid-mounted thermal system developed to combine refrigeration and domestic hot water production.

Its architecture brings together CO₂ (R744), R290, solar energy capture, thermal storage and coordinated control — exploring how active subcooling, solar assistance and thermal storage can work together to improve CO₂ system performance.

How the concept works

  1. 1

    Capture solar energy

    Integrate solar energy into the system’s thermal strategy.

  2. 2

    Store and manage heat

    Use thermal storage and coordinated control to support hot water production.

  3. 3

    Support CO₂ performance

    Integrate an R290 heat pump to provide active subcooling for the CO₂ circuit.

  4. 4

    Deliver refrigeration

    Use the primary CO₂ system to serve refrigerated storage applications.

Designed for efficiency. Developed through engineering.

Performance targets

Targets in the presented comparison of a standard CO₂ system against the Unit 40-12 concept.

COP +31.5% 1.84 → 2.42 Approximately 31.5% improvement in coefficient of performance.
CO₂ discharge pressure −17.6% 9,461 → 7,793 kPa Lower discharge pressure on the CO₂ circuit.
Combined power input −2.99 kW 26.38 → 23.39 kW A reduction of 2.99 kW, including the R290 support system.
